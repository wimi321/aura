# Aura Story Support

Aura Story is a free, private, on-device scenario roleplay app for iPhone.

## Contact

- Email: 281928643@qq.com
- GitHub issues: https://github.com/wimi321/aura/issues

## What to Include

When reporting a problem, please include:

- Device model, for example iPhone 15 Pro or iPhone 11
- iOS version
- Aura Story version
- What you were doing before the problem happened
- Whether the issue is about import, local generation, language display, or the app interface

Please do not include private chat text, imported card content, or personal scenario details unless you intentionally choose to share them for troubleshooting.

## Privacy Note

Aura Story does not require an account. The iOS version runs text generation locally on the user's iPhone. Chats, imported Tavern/SillyTavern roleplay cards, world settings, session history, and generated text are not uploaded by Aura Story.
