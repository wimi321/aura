# App Review Reply Draft: Aura Story Is Not Publication Content

Hello App Review Team,

Thank you for the review. We have updated the app metadata, support URL, category, and review notes to clarify Aura Story's actual product scope.

Aura Story does not distribute books, magazines, periodicals, serialized publications, reading catalogs, chapters, editorial articles, or paid reading content. It has no bookstore, magazine feed, publishing marketplace, content subscription, or paid content unlock. The built-in scenes are lightweight local scenario roleplay starters used to initialize on-device text generation.

Aura Story is a free on-device scenario roleplay app for iPhone. The iOS version works offline after launch and runs text generation locally on the user's iPhone with the bundled on-device model. It does not call third-party AI services, cloud LLM APIs, or remote inference endpoints.

Users may optionally import local Tavern/SillyTavern PNG or JSON roleplay card files from Photos or Files. These files are selected by the user, parsed locally, and are not sold, hosted, distributed, published, uploaded, or unlocked by Aura Story.

Business model:

1. Aura Story is a free app.
2. There is no paid digital content.
3. There is no subscription.
4. There is no in-app purchase.
5. There is no external purchase flow.
6. There is no previously purchased content to access.
7. No paid content or paid features are unlocked outside Apple's In-App Purchase system because the app does not offer paid content or paid features.

For China mainland review, Aura Story should not be treated as a book, magazine, periodical, or publishing service because it does not provide publication content or distribute third-party reading material. It is a local private roleplay tool where user interactions and imported local configuration files remain on the user's own device.
